Google Summer of Code 2018

I recently got accepted into the Google Summer of Code program. In this program, students get the opportunity to work with open-source organizations from all around the world and complete a software project. The work spans almost three months, and we’re paid a very good stipend for it (for students in India, it’s US$2,400).

Tell me what’s cooler than Google funding your summer project. I’ll wait.

What makes this even cooler is that I got accepted in my first year. If you’re from Manipal, you’ll know how pathetic the coding culture is here. As far as I know, only two students from here qualified for GSoC 2018. I could go ahead and start ranting about the lacklustre attitude most students here have towards knowledge and creativity, but I’ll leave that for another post.

As for my GSoC project, I’ll be working with OpenWISP. You can learn more about them on their website, and you can read more about my project in my proposal (PDF).
