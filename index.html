FAQ

Quick answers to the questions people usually ask.

What do you work on day to day?

Backend services, data pipelines, and applied ML and AI agent work for machines in the field: the APIs and systems that sit behind the product.

What does your stack look like?

Mostly Python with FastAPI and Django, on Postgres and AWS, with Terraform for infrastructure and LLMs where they earn their keep.

How did you get into open source?

Through Google Code-in in school, which I won in 2013 and 2014, and then Google Summer of Code 2018 with OpenWISP.

Are you open to collaborations?

Yes. I’m always up for interesting problems, collaborations and conversations. Email is the best way to reach me.

Where can I see your code?

Most of my public work is on GitHub.
